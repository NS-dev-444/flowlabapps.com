FAQ
Quick answers
Do you support presets and custom goals?

Yes—use 14:10, 18:6, 20:4 presets, or set a custom target from 10–24 hours.

Is this just a fasting timer?

No—PulseFast is action-focused: hydration/recovery cues, movement + guardrails, and insight cards that summarize your progress.

Which devices are supported?

Android is the current focus. iOS support is planned. Web/desktop builds may be used for internal testing.

Who owns my data?

Your fasting history, steps, and notes are designed to stay on your device unless you enable cloud features in the future.
