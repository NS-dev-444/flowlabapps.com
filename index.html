FAQ
Quick answers
Do you support presets and custom goals?

Yes—use 14:10, 18:6, 20:4 presets, or set a custom target from 10–24 hours.

Why don’t I see hydration or steps?

They were removed for now after performance feedback. The current builds focus on smooth fasting + insights.

Which devices are supported?

Android is the current focus. iOS support is planned later.
