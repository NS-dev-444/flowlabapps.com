Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Flowlab Apps (“we,” “us”) handles information in the PulseFast app (“PulseFast”).

Plain English: PulseFast is designed to keep your fasting sessions and notes on your device. If we add optional cloud sync or analytics later, this policy will be updated before it applies.

Information we handle

  • On-device app data: fasting sessions, eating windows, goals, history, insight summaries, and notes you add.
  • Support communications: if you email support, you may share screenshots or device details so we can help troubleshoot.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not require an account to use core features (unless added in a future version).

How we use information

  • To provide core app features (timers, history, notes, and insights).
  • To respond to support requests you initiate.

Sharing

We do not share your app data with third parties except when required by law, or if you explicitly enable a third-party integration in a future feature.

Data retention

Your PulseFast data is stored on your device. Removing the app typically removes local app data (subject to your device backup/restore settings).
