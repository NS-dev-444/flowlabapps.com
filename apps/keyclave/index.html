Keyclave

An offline, encrypted vault for the documents that matter — passports, licences, insurance, medical records, titles, warranties and contracts. Nothing leaves your device, because there is nowhere for it to go.

iOSmacOS In development

Overview

Your documents, encrypted and offline.

Every document is encrypted with its own key, and those keys are wrapped by a master key derived from your password. Unlock with a password, a six-digit PIN, Face ID or Touch ID, with a 160-bit recovery key as a fallback.

Text inside your documents is recognized on-device with Apple's Vision framework, so search covers what a document says and not just what you named it. Filter by category, tag, date, expiration and document type.

There is no networking code anywhere in the project — no analytics, no logging, no third-party SDKs and no cloud sync. The privacy claim is a property of the build, not a promise in a policy.

Features

What it does

01Per-file encryption

AES-256-GCM with a per-document key, wrapped by a master key derived from your password.

02Unlock how you like

Password or six-digit PIN, plus optional Face ID and Touch ID, with a recovery key as a fallback.

03On-device OCR

Apple Vision reads the text in your documents so search finds what they actually say.

04Organized how you think

Thirteen categories, unlimited user-defined tags, and filters for date, expiration and type.

05Save to Vault

A share extension so documents can go straight into the vault from anywhere in the system.

06Expiration reminders

Local notifications before a passport, licence or policy runs out. Scheduled on-device.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

Keyclave collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics — and no networking code in the app at all.
