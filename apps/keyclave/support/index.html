Reporting a problem

Please include as much of this as you can — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Device model and OS version
  • What you were doing — importing, searching, unlocking
  • What you expected versus what happened
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Never include the document itself, your password or your recovery key
FAQ

Common questions

What happens if I forget my password?

Use your recovery key. If you have lost both, the vault cannot be opened — by you or by us. Your master password is never stored anywhere, which is what makes the encryption real.

Is my vault backed up to iCloud?

No. There is no cloud sync of any kind. Use the app's own encrypted export if you want a backup, and store it somewhere you control.

Can Flowlab Apps read my documents?

No. We have no server, no account system and no key. There is nothing on our side to read.

Does search work offline?

Yes. Text recognition runs on your device with Apple's Vision framework, and the index is stored encrypted alongside your documents.

How do I add a document from another app?

Use the Share sheet and pick “Save to Vault”.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later, and Mac running macOS 14 or later.

Your data

Privacy and data requests

Keyclave collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics — and no networking code in the app at all.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no server-side data for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device. Full detail is in the Keyclave privacy policy.
