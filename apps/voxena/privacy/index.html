Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026 · Applies to Voxena for iOS, iPadOS, Android, all versions

The short version. Voxena keeps your recordings, notes and documents on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no Voxena server, and the app makes no network requests of its own apart from speech-model downloads you start.

What we collect What is stored Permissions Network Third parties Store declarations Children Your rights Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Flowlab Apps (“we”, “us”) handles information in Voxena (the “app”). It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address collected, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. No SDK in the app reports your behaviour to anyone.

Flowlab Apps operates no server that holds your Voxena data, because no such data ever reaches us. That is not a policy decision we could quietly reverse in an update — there is no backend to send it to.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything Voxena saves is stored on the device you are using it on.

WhatWhereWhy
RecordingsOn your deviceThe audio you record or import
Transcripts, notes and dictationOn your deviceThe text you create, with its raw version kept alongside any Tidy changes
Imported documents and their extracted textOn your deviceSo they can be read aloud and searched
Search index, folders and tagsOn your deviceTo find and organise your library
Prepared speech audioOn your device, in a cache you can clearSo a document you prepared plays without re-rendering
Settings, dictionary and pronunciation rulesOn your deviceTo remember how you like Voxena to work

3. Permissions

Voxena asks for the following, and nothing else. Every permission marked optional can be declined, and the app keeps working without it.

  • Microphone — Only when you tap Record or start dictation. Audio is stored on your device and never uploaded by Voxena.
  • Speech recognition (iPhone and iPad) — Lets iOS turn your speech into text. Voxena requires on-device recognition, so the audio is not sent to Apple's servers.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint (optional) — Only if you turn on Lock Voxena. The check happens in the operating system; Voxena never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications (Android) — Android shows a notification while recording or reading aloud continues in the background. Voxena sends no other notifications.
  • Background audio — So recording and reading aloud continue with the screen locked.

4. Network activity

Voxena has no backend and sends none of your content anywhere. The only network traffic it can cause is a speech-model download you start: on iPhone and iPad, iOS fetches Apple's on-device speech model from Apple; on Android, Voxena downloads the model file you choose from the open-source sherpa-onnx releases on GitHub and checks it against a pinned SHA-256 fingerprint. No personal data is sent with either request. On Android, a few text-to-speech engines offer voices that work online; Voxena marks them needs network and never picks one automatically, and if you choose one yourself, the text being read goes to that engine's service. Opening a licence link from Settings opens your browser.

5. Third parties

None receive your content. Apple (iOS speech models) and GitHub (Android speech models) serve the model files you ask to download and receive only the ordinary request for that file. There are no analytics libraries, crash reporters or advertising SDKs.

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information, because we do not hold any of it.

6. What we declare to the app stores

Apple and Google each require a privacy declaration alongside the app listing. These are the answers we give for Voxena, reproduced here so you can check them against the rest of this policy.

Store declarationOur answer
Apple App Store — App Privacy Data Not Collected. Nothing is collected from this app, by us or by anyone else, so no data type is linked to you and none is used to track you.
Google Play — Data safety No data collected. No data shared with third parties. No data is transmitted off the device, so there is none to encrypt in transit. Users can erase everything the app holds by deleting it.
Account deletion Not applicable — the app has no account. See Data & Account Deletion.

Your library can be included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) if you turn that on during setup or in Settings › Privacy. It is off until you choose. Those backups are made by your device's backup service under its own terms; Voxena does not upload them and we never receive them. That is why the declarations above still read no data collected.

7. Children

Voxena is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone of any age. Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children.

8. Additional notes

Settings › Privacy lists every feature that could move data off the device, generated from the same declaration as the store labels above. Settings › Delete all data erases every recording, note and document at once, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days unless you empty it sooner. Voxena is not a meeting tool: it does not identify speakers, and you are responsible for having permission to record the people you record where the law requires it.

9. Your rights and your choices

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. Flowlab Apps holds no personal data about Voxena users, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase on request.

You remain in full control of the data on your own device:

  • Delete individual items from inside the app.
  • Remove the app to delete its local data, subject to your device's own backup and restore settings.
  • Revoke any permission at any time from your device's system settings.

We do not sell personal information and never have. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security rests on your device's own protections — your passcode, your biometric lock and your operating system's storage encryption. Keep your device updated and locked.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of Voxena ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent in-app.
