Voxena

Voxena turns speech into text and text into speech without anything leaving your iPhone or iPad. Record a lecture or a memo and watch the words appear as you talk, dictate a note, or import a PDF, Word document or e-book and have it read aloud with the sentence highlighted as it goes.

Overview

Record, read and listen — all on your iPhone or iPad.

Recognition runs on the device, using the speech engine built into iOS or, on Android, a model you download once. Recording keeps going with the screen locked, and a “phone on a table” mode picks up a lecture or a room more evenly than voice processing tuned for one close speaker.

Listen reads any note, transcript or document with the voices installed on your device, highlighting each sentence, and carries on from the Lock Screen. Mixed-language documents switch voices sentence by sentence, right-to-left scripts such as Persian and Arabic read in the right order, and a pronunciation dictionary fixes names a voice gets wrong. Prepare a whole document ahead of time and it plays offline.

Everything lands in one library with folders, tags and full-text search. Tidy removes filler words and adds punctuation without rewording what you said, and the raw text is always kept. Export any item as plain text, Markdown, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or the original audio, or export the whole library as an archive.

Voxena is not a meeting app: it does not identify speakers, join calls or send anything to a cloud service. There is no account and no subscription. It comes to iPhone and iPad first; the Android version follows on Google Play later.

Features

What it does

01Record with live text

Transcripts appear as you speak, or afterwards from the audio. Recording continues with the screen locked.

02Read aloud

Notes, transcripts, PDFs, Word files, EPUB books and web pages read with your device's voices, sentence by sentence, from the Lock Screen too.

03Documents in, cleanly

Headings, lists and tables survive import. Scanned PDFs are recognised on iPhone and iPad, and right-to-left text reads in order.

04Dictate and Tidy

Speak into a note. Tidy removes fillers and adds punctuation without changing your words, and a personal dictionary fixes names.

05One searchable library

Folders, tags and full-text search across every recording, note and document, including languages written without spaces.

06Private by design

No account, no analytics and no Voxena cloud. An optional app lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your passcode.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

Voxena keeps your recordings, notes and documents on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no Voxena server, and the app makes no network requests of its own apart from speech-model downloads you start.
