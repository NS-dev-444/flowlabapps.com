Voxena Support

Voxena turns speech into text and text into speech without anything leaving your iPhone or iPad. Record a lecture or a memo and watch the words appear as you talk, dictate a note, or import a PDF, Word document or e-book and have it read aloud with the sentence highlighted as it goes.

Reporting a problem

Please include as much of this as you can — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Device model and OS version
  • The Voxena version (Settings › About)
  • What you were doing — recording, importing, reading aloud — and the file type if it was an import
  • What you expected versus what happened
  • If you are comfortable sharing it, the diagnostics report from Settings › Diagnostics, which contains no recordings or text
FAQ

Common questions

Does Voxena need an internet connection?

No. Recording, transcription, reading aloud, import and search all work offline. The only download is a speech model you choose to fetch.

Which languages can it transcribe?

The languages your device's on-device speech engine supports for the language you pick in Settings › Speech recognition. On Android, the downloaded model sets the language.

Which files can I import?

PDF (including scanned pages on iPhone and iPad), Word (.docx), EPUB without DRM, HTML, plain text, Markdown, and common audio formats. You can also open a file in Voxena from another app's share sheet.

Why does a voice sound robotic?

Voxena uses the voices installed on your device. On iPhone and iPad, download Enhanced or Premium voices in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices and Voxena uses them automatically.

Is my library backed up?

Only if you choose to include it in your device's own backups during setup or in Settings › Privacy. You can also export everything as an archive from Settings › Export everything.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or subscription.

Your data

Privacy and data requests

Voxena keeps your recordings, notes and documents on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no Voxena server, and the app makes no network requests of its own apart from speech-model downloads you start.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no server-side data for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device. Full detail is in the Voxena privacy policy.
