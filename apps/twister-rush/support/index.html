Reporting a problem

Please include as much of this as you can — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Device model and OS version
  • What you were absorbing when it happened
  • What you expected versus what happened
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Whether it is reproducible or intermittent
FAQ

Common questions

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including the five rival tornadoes, runs on your device.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, neither.

Why won't a building go into my tornado?

It is a tier above you. Grow first, or knock it flat — a felled object needs 20% less tornado when you return.

How long is a match?

Two minutes, after a short countdown, ending on a ranked results screen with Play Again.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets.

Your data

Privacy and data requests

Twister Rush is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no server-side data for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device. Full detail is in the Twister Rush privacy policy.
