Twister Rush

A 3D arcade tornado-growth game. Drive a funnel through a city, absorb everything you are big enough to lift, and climb a twelve-tier ladder from drink cups to skyscrapers.

iOSiPadOSAndroid In development
Support Privacy policy Email us
Overview

Twelve tiers, from drink cups to skyscrapers.

What you can absorb is decided by how big an object actually is — measured off its mesh rather than read from a category someone assigned — so two things the same size always behave the same way, and a bigger thing is always harder.

Objects one tier above you do not sit still. They heave up, tip toward the funnel and drop back, and every so often one gives way. When one will not go, you can knock it flat instead — and a felled object needs 20% less tornado when you come back for it.

Five AI tornadoes share the city, colour-coded by archetype, and a crown floats over whichever funnel is currently the biggest, so you always know whether that is you.

Features

What it does

01Twelve-tier ladder

From drink cups to skyscrapers, with absorption decided by real mesh size.

02Two-minute matches

A 3·2·1·GO countdown, a two-minute match, a ranked results screen and Play Again with no menu in between.

03Five rival tornadoes

Colour-coded AI archetypes that hunt, harvest and avoid differently.

04Objects that resist

Buildings heave and tip before they go. Knock one flat and it is easier next time.

05The crown

Floats over the biggest tornado on the map so the standings are always readable at a glance.

06One codebase, two stores

A single Unity project targeting iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

Twister Rush is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.
