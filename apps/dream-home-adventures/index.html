Dream Home Adventures

A 2.5D dollhouse game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Customize an avatar, move into a starter apartment, and decorate it room by room with real 3D furniture you place yourself.

iOSiPadOSmacOS In development
Support Privacy policy Email us
Overview

Decorate, collect, and make it yours.

Enter Decorate mode, select a piece of furniture, drag it into the room, rotate it, and confirm the placement when it reads green. Change wall and floor finishes, interact with household objects, and work through short tasks that open up more of the house.

Walk into the Dream Decor Store, buy something, carry it home and place it from your inventory. Progress saves automatically after purchases, placements, tasks, room exits and whenever the app goes to the background.

There are no ads, no open chat, no real-money purchases, no energy gates and no punitive pet systems. It is a game about arranging a room you like.

Features

What it does

01Real 3D placement

Drag, rotate and confirm furniture in a controlled dollhouse camera — not a grid of flat stickers.

02Walk-in store

Enter the Dream Decor Store, buy an item, return home and place it from inventory.

03Finishes and interactions

Change wall and floor finishes, and interact with household objects around the home.

04Tasks and properties

Short tasks and multiple properties driven by reusable catalogs.

05Autosave

Progress is written after purchases, placements, tasks, room exits and app backgrounding.

06No dark patterns

No ads, no open chat, no real-money purchases, no energy gates, no punitive pet systems.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

Dream Home Adventures is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.
