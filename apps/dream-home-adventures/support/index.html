Reporting a problem

Please include as much of this as you can — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Device model and OS version
  • Which room and which piece of furniture
  • What you expected versus what happened
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Whether progress was lost, and roughly when it last saved
FAQ

Common questions

Are there real-money purchases?

No. In-game currency is earned by playing, and there is nothing to buy with real money.

Is there chat or online play?

No. The game is entirely single-player and offline.

Why won't a piece of furniture place?

Placement must read green. Rotate it or move it clear of another object and the indicator will change.

Is my progress saved automatically?

Yes — after purchases, placements, tasks, room exits and whenever the app goes to the background.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later, in landscape, plus Mac.

Your data

Privacy and data requests

Dream Home Adventures is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no server-side data for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device. Full detail is in the Dream Home Adventures privacy policy.
