Dream Home Adventures

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Applies to Dream Home Adventures for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, all versions

The short version. Dream Home Adventures is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.

What we collect What is stored Permissions Network Third parties Store declarations Children Your rights Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Flowlab Apps (“we”, “us”) handles information in Dream Home Adventures (the “app”). It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address collected, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. No SDK in the app reports your behaviour to anyone.

Flowlab Apps operates no server that holds your Dream Home Adventures data, because no such data ever reaches us. That is not a policy decision we could quietly reverse in an update — there is no backend to send it to.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything Dream Home Adventures saves is stored on the device you are using it on.

WhatWhereWhy
Your avatar, homes and furniture placementsOn your deviceSo your home is exactly as you left it
Inventory, currency and task progressOn your deviceTo restore your progress
Settings and preferencesOn your deviceTo keep the game configured how you left it

3. Permissions

Dream Home Adventures asks for the following, and nothing else. Every permission marked optional can be declined, and the app keeps working without it.

  • Add to Photo Library (optional) — Only if you choose to save a picture of a room you built. The app can add that image to your photos; it cannot read your photo library.

4. Network activity

The game does not connect to the internet. There is no account, no cloud save and no online play.

5. Third parties

None. No advertising networks, analytics SDKs, crash reporters or third-party trackers are included.

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information, because we do not hold any of it.

6. What we declare to the app stores

Apple requires a privacy declaration alongside the app listing. These are the answers we give for Dream Home Adventures, reproduced here so you can check them against the rest of this policy.

Store declarationOur answer
Apple App Store — App Privacy Data Not Collected. Nothing is collected from this app, by us or by anyone else, so no data type is linked to you and none is used to track you.
Account deletion Not applicable — the app has no account. See Data & Account Deletion.

7. Purchases

There are no real-money purchases. In-game currency is earned by playing and cannot be bought.

8. Children

Dream Home Adventures is suitable for children. It collects no data from anyone of any age. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no open chat and no user-generated content shared with other players.

9. Your rights and your choices

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. Flowlab Apps holds no personal data about Dream Home Adventures users, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase on request.

You remain in full control of the data on your own device:

  • Delete individual items from inside the app.
  • Remove the app to delete its local data, subject to your device's own backup and restore settings.
  • Revoke any permission at any time from your device's system settings.

We do not sell personal information and never have. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security rests on your device's own protections — your passcode, your biometric lock and your operating system's storage encryption. Keep your device updated and locked.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of Dream Home Adventures ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent in-app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in any Flowlab Apps app:

  • Email: dev@flowlabapps.com
  • Support: flowlabapps.com/apps/dream-home-adventures/support/

We aim to answer within a few business days.
