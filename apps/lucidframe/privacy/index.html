LucidFrame

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Applies to LucidFrame for macOS, all versions

The short version. LucidFrame collects nothing. It ships sandboxed with no networking entitlement, so it is technically incapable of making a network connection at all.

What we collect What is stored Permissions Network Third parties Store declarations Children Your rights Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Flowlab Apps (“we”, “us”) handles information in LucidFrame (the “app”). It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address collected, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. No SDK in the app reports your behaviour to anyone.

Flowlab Apps operates no server that holds your LucidFrame data, because no such data ever reaches us. That is not a policy decision we could quietly reverse in an update — there is no backend to send it to.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything LucidFrame saves is stored on the device you are using it on.

WhatWhereWhy
Your capturesThe app's sandbox container on your MacSo the library is still there next launch
Annotations and capture metadataAn index file in the same containerSo edits remain re-editable
Preferences (hotkeys, history limit, capture options)macOS user defaults on this MacTo remember how you like the app configured
A bookmark to your chosen save foldermacOS user defaults on this MacOnly if you turn on “Also save to folder”, so it can keep writing there after a restart

3. Permissions

LucidFrame asks for the following, and nothing else. Every permission marked optional can be declined, and the app keeps working without it.

  • Screen Recording — macOS requires this permission of any app that captures the screen. LucidFrame uses it solely to take the screenshots you ask for, when you ask for them. Nothing is captured in the background.
  • Folder access (optional) — Only the one folder you pick yourself through the standard macOS panel, and only if you enable saving copies to a folder.

4. Network activity

None, and this is enforced rather than promised: the app ships without the network entitlement, so macOS itself blocks any outgoing or incoming connection whether or not the code attempts one. There is no server for your screenshots to go to.

5. Third parties

None. No third-party SDKs, advertising libraries, trackers or analytics frameworks are compiled into the app.

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information, because we do not hold any of it.

6. What we declare to the app stores

Apple and Google each require a privacy declaration alongside the app listing. These are the answers we give for LucidFrame, reproduced here so you can check them against the rest of this policy.

Store declarationOur answer
Apple App Store — App Privacy Data Not Collected. Nothing is collected from this app, by us or by anyone else, so no data type is linked to you and none is used to track you.
Google Play — Data safety No data collected. No data shared with third parties. No data is transmitted off the device, so there is none to encrypt in transit. Users can erase everything the app holds by deleting it.
Account deletion Not applicable — the app has no account. See Data & Account Deletion.

7. Children

LucidFrame is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone of any age. Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children.

8. Additional notes

LucidFrame does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendars or photo library, and cannot read any file you have not explicitly opened.

9. Your rights and your choices

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. Flowlab Apps holds no personal data about LucidFrame users, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase on request.

You remain in full control of the data on your own device:

  • Delete individual items from inside the app.
  • Remove the app to delete its local data, subject to your device's own backup and restore settings.
  • Revoke any permission at any time from your device's system settings.

We do not sell personal information and never have. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security rests on your device's own protections — your passcode, your biometric lock and your operating system's storage encryption. Keep your device updated and locked.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of LucidFrame ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent in-app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in any Flowlab Apps app:

  • Email: dev@flowlabapps.com
  • Support: flowlabapps.com/apps/lucidframe/support/

We aim to answer within a few business days.
