LucidFrame

LucidFrame is a screenshot tool that lives in your menu bar and stays out of your way. Press a key, grab what you need, mark it up, and get on with your work.

macOS Store submission prep
Support Privacy policy Email us
Overview

Menu bar screenshots and markup.

Capture a region, a single window or a whole display from a global shortcut — ⌃⇧3, ⌃⇧4 and ⌃⇧5 out of the box, remappable to whatever you prefer. Window captures come out with a proper macOS drop shadow and padding, so they look finished without extra work.

Every capture lands in your clipboard immediately and appears as a floating thumbnail you can click to edit or drag straight into another app. Annotations — arrows, boxes, highlights, text labels and crops — stay editable rather than being baked into the pixels.

Redaction is real redaction: the covered pixels are destroyed in the exported image, not painted over with a rectangle someone can move aside.

Features

What it does

01Menu bar, no dock icon

Launches instantly, does no idle work, and never takes up a slot in your dock.

02Global shortcuts

Region, window and display capture from the keyboard, with remappable hotkeys.

03Zero-click flow

Capture, auto-copy to clipboard, floating thumbnail — click to edit, drag to drop the file elsewhere.

04Editable annotations

Arrows, boxes, highlights, text and crop. Move, re-edit or delete anything you have already drawn.

05Proper redaction

Redacted pixels are destroyed in the export, not merely covered.

06Searchable library

A keyboard-navigable grid of past captures with Quick Look, search, drag-out and Reveal in Finder.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

LucidFrame collects nothing. It ships sandboxed with no networking entitlement, so it is technically incapable of making a network connection at all.
