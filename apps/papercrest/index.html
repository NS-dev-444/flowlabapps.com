PaperCrest

PaperCrest scans, straightens, enhances and reads your documents entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. It has no account, no analytics and no way to reach the internet: the iOS app contains no networking code, and the Android app holds no network permission.

Overview

Scan documents that never leave your device.

The app opens on the camera. It finds the page in the frame, captures it when it is steady, and keeps going for as many pages as you need, with guided modes for an ID card (front and back on one sheet) and a passport. Review lets you crop, rotate and enhance each page, choosing Automatic, Document, Black & White, Grayscale, Photo or ID / Card, and you can import photos and PDFs through the system picker instead of scanning. On iPad the library sits in a column beside the camera rather than in a sheet over it.

After you save, your device reads the text, works out what kind of document it is from around thirty types (passport, receipt, bank statement, contract, business card and more), and titles it from what the page actually says, never from the date you scanned it. A receipt's merchant, date and total, or a business card's name, phone and email, are picked out as suggestions you can correct, and a business card can go straight to Contacts through the share sheet. Everything is searchable, with folders, tags, favourites and a Trash you can restore from.

Sensitive values such as card numbers, national ID numbers, passport machine-readable zones and dates of birth are detected on the page and offered for redaction, which destroys those pixels in the copy you share and leaves the original untouched. Protect encrypts a document with AES-256 behind Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your passcode, and an optional App Lock guards the whole library. Exported PDFs are searchable, carry only the document's title, and nothing about you or your device.

Recognition, classification and extraction all run on the device with Apple's Vision framework or Google's bundled ML Kit model; nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent. Version 1.0 runs on iPhone, iPad and Android phones. On iPhone and iPad it reads text in the languages your device prefers; on Android it reads any Latin-script language. There is no account and no subscription.

Features

What it does

01Camera first

Opens on the viewfinder. Live page detection, auto-capture and multi-page scans, with guided modes for ID cards and passports.

02Clean pages

Crop, rotate and enhance: Automatic, Document, Black & White, Grayscale, Photo and ID / Card. Import photos and PDFs too.

03Reads and files itself

On-device text recognition, a document type, a title taken from the page, and a receipt's total or a business card's details, all correctable.

04Find anything

Full-text search across your unprotected documents, plus folders, tags, favourites and a Trash you can restore from.

05Redact and protect

Detected card numbers, ID numbers and dates of birth are offered for redaction, which removes the pixels from the shared copy. Protect encrypts a document with AES-256 behind Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode.

06Cannot phone home

No networking code on iOS and no network permission on Android, checked by the build. No account, no analytics, no ads.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

PaperCrest collects nothing and cannot send anything. The iOS app contains no networking code and the Android app has no network permission, so your scans, their text and everything the app works out about them stay on your device.
