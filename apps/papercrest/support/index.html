PaperCrest Support

PaperCrest scans, straightens, enhances and reads your documents entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. It has no account, no analytics and no way to reach the internet: the iOS app contains no networking code, and the Android app holds no network permission.

Reporting a problem

Please include as much of this as you can — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix:

  • Device model and OS version
  • The PaperCrest version (Settings)
  • What you were doing: scanning, importing, exporting, redacting or protecting, and the file type if it was an import
  • What you expected versus what happened
  • Please do not attach the document itself unless it contains nothing private. A description of the kind of page is enough
FAQ

Common questions

Does PaperCrest need an internet connection?

No. Scanning, text recognition, classification, search, redaction, protection and export all work offline, and the app has no way to go online: there is no networking code in the iOS app and no network permission in the Android app.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, and Android phones on Android 8.0 or later. On iPad the library sits beside the viewfinder rather than over it.

Which languages does it read?

On iPhone and iPad, the languages your device prefers, from those Apple's Vision framework can recognise, and English is always included. On Android, the bundled ML Kit model reads any language written in the Latin script.

What can I import?

Photos and PDFs, through the system picker. Imported pages are enhanced, read and classified just like scans.

Does redaction really remove the text?

Yes. Redaction is applied to a copy: the marked regions are destroyed in the pixels of every page, and the copy is exported as images only, with no text layer. The original in your library is unchanged.

What does Protect do, and what if I lose my passcode?

Protect encrypts the document's pages, text and details with AES-256 behind Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or your passcode. A protected document is not in search results, because its text is encrypted rather than hidden. If you lose the credential that guards it, it cannot be recovered; there is no way around this, by design.

Will my documents come back on a new phone?

On iPhone and iPad, unprotected documents are part of your normal device backup and restore with it; protected documents do not, so remove protection before you move if you want to keep them. On Android nothing in the app is backed up by the system, so export what you need first.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or subscription.

Your data

Privacy and data requests

PaperCrest collects nothing and cannot send anything. The iOS app contains no networking code and the Android app has no network permission, so your scans, their text and everything the app works out about them stay on your device.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no account to close and no server-side data for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device. Full detail is in the PaperCrest privacy policy.
