City Rush

A top-down arcade driving game with two ways to play on the same city: an endless Rush Hour score attack, and a twelve-mission Career.

iOSiPadOS In development
Support Privacy policy Email us
Overview

Pick up a fare. Drop it off. Go again.

Rush Hour is one loop with no menus in the middle of it. A shift starts at 45 seconds, and reaching a pickup or completing a drop-off banks time sized to the leg you just drove — so a long haul funds itself and a short hop does not. The run ends when the clock does.

Every delivery raises a streak multiplier up to ×10. Every crash resets it to ×1 and costs two seconds, which makes driving clean the actual skill. Finish a fare without touching anything and it pays 25% extra before the multiplier applies.

Ambient traffic circulates the grid on correct right-hand lanes and picks turns at intersections. Hitting one is a crash; sliding past one at speed is a near miss, which pays cash and banks nitro. Coins sit in the driving lanes, so the greedy line and the fast line rarely match.

Features

What it does

01Rush Hour

An endless score-attack shift. Two taps from launch to driving, and it lasts as long as you keep earning time.

02Career

Twelve stage-driven missions on the same city, reached from the home screen.

03Streak multiplier

Up to ×10 for clean driving, reset to ×1 by any crash.

04Near misses

Sliding past traffic at speed pays cash and banks nitro. Hitting it does not.

05Emergent session length

A bad shift is 40 seconds, a good one runs several minutes. Nothing asks you to commit up front.

06Difficulty that ramps

Time is granted less generously and traffic thickens with every delivery, instead of a hard timer cutting you off.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

City Rush is an offline game. It collects no data, shows no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.
