Black Hole Rush

A fast-session arcade growth game. You are a small black hole loose in Gravity City. Everything you swallow makes you bigger, and everything bigger than you is a threat — until it isn't.

Overview

Start small. Swallow the city.

A match runs two minutes across three phases — Scavenge, Expansion and Collapse — and then a thirty-second sudden-death Elimination round where a single point of mass is enough to swallow a rival and nobody who goes down comes back.

Six seeded districts, five personality-driven bots, and capture rules that are exactly as strict as they sound: you need a 0.1% radius edge, so only a near-exact tie bounces.

Everything unlockable is cosmetic. There is no pay-to-win system, no energy gate and no timer standing between you and the next match — rematch happens without a reload.

Features

What it does

01Three-minute sessions

A two-minute three-phase match plus a thirty-second elimination round. Built for the gap between other things.

02Six districts

Gravity City is seeded into six districts with different object density and risk.

03Five rival bots

Each with its own personality — they hunt, hoard and avoid differently.

04Gravity Pulse

A short-cooldown ability that shoves nearby mass, on a button and on the space bar.

05Cosmetics only

Progression, challenges and equippable cosmetics. No pay-to-win, no loot boxes, no energy gates.

06Accessibility

Reduced motion, high contrast, text size, handedness, camera shake, and separate music, effects and haptics controls.

Privacy at a glance

This app collects no data

Black Hole Rush is a completely offline game. It collects no data, contains no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.
