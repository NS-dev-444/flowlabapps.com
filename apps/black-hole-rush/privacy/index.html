Black Hole Rush

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Applies to Black Hole Rush for iOS, iPadOS, Android, all versions

The short version. Black Hole Rush is a completely offline game. It collects no data, contains no advertising, has no analytics and makes no network connections.

What we collect What is stored Permissions Network Third parties Store declarations Children Your rights Contact

This Privacy Policy explains how Flowlab Apps (“we”, “us”) handles information in Black Hole Rush (the “app”). It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address collected, no advertising identifier, no usage analytics and no crash reporting. No SDK in the app reports your behaviour to anyone.

Flowlab Apps operates no server that holds your Black Hole Rush data, because no such data ever reaches us. That is not a policy decision we could quietly reverse in an update — there is no backend to send it to.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything Black Hole Rush saves is stored on the device you are using it on.

WhatWhereWhy
Progression, XP and currencyOn your deviceSo your unlocks are still there next session
Challenges and journey milestonesOn your deviceTo track what you have completed
Equipped cosmetics and statisticsOn your deviceTo restore your setup and show your record
Settings and tutorial completionOn your deviceSo the game stays configured how you left it

3. Permissions

Black Hole Rush asks for the following, and nothing else. Every permission marked optional can be declined, and the app keeps working without it.

  • None — The game requests no runtime permissions. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

4. Network activity

The game does not connect to the internet. There is no account, no leaderboard server, no cloud save and no matchmaking service — the rival holes are bots running on your own device.

5. Third parties

None. No advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters, no social login and no third-party trackers are included in the build.

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your personal information, because we do not hold any of it.

6. What we declare to the app stores

Apple and Google each require a privacy declaration alongside the app listing. These are the answers we give for Black Hole Rush, reproduced here so you can check them against the rest of this policy.

Store declarationOur answer
Apple App Store — App Privacy Data Not Collected. Nothing is collected from this app, by us or by anyone else, so no data type is linked to you and none is used to track you.
Google Play — Data safety No data collected. No data shared with third parties. No data is transmitted off the device, so there is none to encrypt in transit. Users can erase everything the app holds by deleting it.
Account deletion Not applicable — the app has no account. See Data & Account Deletion.

7. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions of any kind.

8. Children

Black Hole Rush is suitable for a broad audience including children. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children — there is nothing to disclose, share or delete. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat and no user-generated content.

9. Your rights and your choices

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. Flowlab Apps holds no personal data about Black Hole Rush users, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct or erase on request.

You remain in full control of the data on your own device:

  • Delete individual items from inside the app.
  • Remove the app to delete its local data, subject to your device's own backup and restore settings.
  • Revoke any permission at any time from your device's system settings.

We do not sell personal information and never have. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

10. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security rests on your device's own protections — your passcode, your biometric lock and your operating system's storage encryption. Keep your device updated and locked.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. If a future version of Black Hole Rush ever begins collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent in-app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in any Flowlab Apps app:

  • Email: dev@flowlabapps.com
  • Support: flowlabapps.com/apps/black-hole-rush/support/

We aim to answer within a few business days.
